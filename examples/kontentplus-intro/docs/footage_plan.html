Framing

What the module shows

1440 × 678 · 2.12:1
+ reach

Era plates are masked into a 1440 × 678 window centred at x 240–1680, y 96–774 of the 1080p frame. A 16:9 source loses roughly 20 % top and bottom, so keep the action in the middle band and leave headroom. 4:3 archival is fine: we scale it to fill the width and crop the height, which is where the era look helps.

  • Kinetic cards ride the lower-left of the module: keep that corner quiet.
  • Faces are welcome now. The generated takes avoided them; real footage should not.
  • Grade neutral. The film applies its own contrast, saturation and era tint in the composition.
  • Motion: slow and continuous. The push between eras adds its own movement.
Route

Archival for the past, one shoot for now

Recommended archival + shoot

Real period footage is the most authentic thing we can put in the module and is exactly what the eras claim: "this is what marketing looked like". Four licensed clips for 1900s–1990s, then a half-day shoot in Stockholm for the 2010s desk and the results scene.

  • Authenticity solves the "stock-like" note outright.
  • Roughly one week end to end, most of it search and licensing.
  • Budget in the low thousands of euros, detailed below.

Alternative period shoot

Stage four eras with props and costume. Full control of action and framing, but a market stall, a letterpress shop, a 1960s boardroom and a mail room are four locations, four wardrobes and two or three shoot days.

  • Only worth it if the film needs a recurring on-camera character across eras.
  • Three to four weeks, budget five to ten times the archival route.

Stockholm shoot

One half-day, two setups

Camera

  • Full-frame mirrorless, UHD 3840×2160, 24 fps, 1/48 shutter
  • Log profile, 10-bit if available
  • 35 mm and 50 mm primes, f/2–2.8
  • Slider or gimbal for the push-ins; no handheld shake

Light

  • Window daylight as key, one soft fill opposite
  • Desk setup: let the monitors light the face for setup B
  • Practical desk lamp on for depth, no coloured gels

Screens

  • Playback files supplied by us (dashboards for the desk, results loop for the wall)
  • Set displays to 60 Hz; check for flicker and moiré at the monitor
  • Wall display brightness one stop under the room

Talent & wardrobe

  • Desk: one marketer, 30s, plain knit or shirt
  • Results: marketing lead + CEO or CMO, contemporary, muted colours
  • No logos, no white shirts against the wall display

Takes

  • Three takes per setup, 10–15 s each, action starts before the slate
  • Setup A over the shoulder, setup B front-on, results wide + one tighter alternate
  • Clean plates of every screen

Delivery

  • ProRes 422 HQ or H.264 100 Mb/s, original resolution
  • Ungraded log plus a one-light reference grade
  • File names: era2010_A_t1.mov, results_wide_t2.mov
Archival specs

What to license

Technical

  • 1080p or better; 2K scans of film are ideal
  • Any frame rate: we conform to 24 fps
  • 4:3 accepted (it fills the module well); 16:9 also fine
  • Prefer clips with the original grain and slight damage over "restored and stabilised": the film tint is added by us
  • Minimum length per slot as in the shot list, ideally 10 s so we can choose the moment

Licence

  • Web, social and events, worldwide, perpetual
  • Editorial-only clips are not enough for a launch film: it needs commercial use
  • Prelinger and other public-domain sources: no licence, but keep the source URL and a note in the project
  • Keep invoices and licence PDFs in kontentplus-intro/refs/licences/

Swap-in

How a clip enters the cut

The build reads plates by name from film/assets/clips/. A new plate only has to be conformed and dropped in under the existing name; the spec, overlays and music alignment rebuild from the narration timings.

  1. Conform to 1920×1080, 24 fps, constant frame rate. For 4:3 sources scale to width and centre-crop.
  2. Save under the slot's name: broll_merchant1900.mp4, ref_1926.mp4, broll_boardroom1960.mp4, broll_mailroom1990.mp4, broll_saasdesk2010.mp4, ref_2016b.mp4, scene_results.mp4.
  3. If the best moment is not at the head of the clip, set mediaStart for that slot in make_spec_v13.py.
  4. Remove the per-era CSS grade that compensated for the generated takes (the 1990s sepia in overlays_v13.py); real footage carries its own look.
  5. Run the chain and render a draft; the pushes stay on their lines because slot lengths come from the narration, not from the clips.
ffmpeg -i in.mov -vf "scale=1920:1080:force_original_aspect_ratio=increase,crop=1920:1080,fps=24" \
  -c:v libx264 -crf 16 -pix_fmt yuv420p -an film/assets/clips/broll_boardroom1960.mp4
cd film && MUSIC=bgmusic_jazz.mp3 python3 make_spec_v13.py && python3 align_music.py 21.99 49.73 \

  && python3 overlays_v13.py && npx hyperframes@0.8.27 check --no-contrast \
  && render_hyperframes.sh . renders/timeline_v13.mp4 --quality draft
