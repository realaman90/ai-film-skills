# "Knowing the customer" — the TIMELINE device (Gemini's read of cut B, edited into a plan)

## Gemini's verdict on cut B v1
The arc (fragmented marketing → one operator) works. What breaks it: the era footage doesn't sit in one visual system
(aspect ratios jump, the marketplace reads "biblical" not "marketing"), the 2010s take has a duplicate-hands artifact at
17 s, the product section spends too long on reaction shots, and the coffee toast dilutes the B2B close.

## The device
A single luminous horizontal line on a dark neutral field. The camera travels along it left → right. Each era is a
rectangular module docked to a decade tick (1900s · 1920s · 1960s · 1990s · 2010s); the era footage lives INSIDE the module,
so every era shares one frame and one motion. Kinetic cards ride with the module (+ REACH · – FACES). At "So we built an
operator" the line pulses, turns brand blue, blooms outward into the workspace, and KontentPlus locks in as the current
marker. The "now" section breaks out of the module into full frame.
